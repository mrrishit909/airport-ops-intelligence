Airport Operations Intelligence

An invented hub at 16:00. A thunderstorm is about to close a runway: who will be late, who will miss a connection, and which gates and holds limit the damage? …
The airport, its timetable, passengers and the day's delays are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real airport. The replay in step 8 uses the simulator's hidden version of the day. Passenger-minute prices are stated assumptions. No outside model or service is called.
